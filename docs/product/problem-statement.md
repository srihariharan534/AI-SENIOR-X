# AI-SENIOR-X — Problem Statement

## The Crisis in Online Technical Education

Modern online learning platforms (MOOCs, tutorial sites, video libraries) suffer from three critical structural failure modes:

### 1. One-Size-Fits-All Static Curriculum
Traditional platforms present static video playlists in a linear sequence. They cannot discern if a learner already possesses strong Python foundations or if they lack fundamental linear algebra. Advanced learners waste time on trivial syntax, while struggling learners encounter insurmountable prerequisite barriers and drop out.

### 2. Superficial "Cheat" Chatbots vs True Pedagogical Guidance
Generic LLM chat interfaces suffer from cognitive passivity. When asked a technical question, they simply output the complete solution or hallucinate plausible code. They do not maintain a persistent model of what the learner knows, do not engage in Socratic questioning, and fail to diagnose *why* the learner struggled.

### 3. Invisible Misconceptions & Lack of Formative Remediation
When a student makes a subtle conceptual error (such as confusing `INNER JOIN` matching with `LEFT JOIN` NULL preservation, or misinterpreting Sigmoid saturation), typical platforms merely show "Incorrect - Try Again". Without diagnosing the specific underlying misconception, students repeatedly practice incorrect patterns.
